What we are

A software product studio. Two engineers, one backlog, and nobody between you and the people writing the code.

We work with founders, CTOs, product and platform teams — technical buyers who have been sold to badly before. The answer to that is specificity, not enthusiasm.

What we are not: an agency renting out hours, a consultancy that leaves slides behind, or an AI startup.

Two people is the constraint and the point. We take one engagement at a time and say no to the second while the first is running. You get the engineers who wrote the code. You do not get a team of ten next month.

What we ship

  • Taimu

    Time tracking for independent contractors.

    A macOS menu-bar app. Start the day, pause, resume, end the day — from the menu bar. A monthly calendar shows each day's intervals, a weekly balance per row, and a monthly total against your goal.

    No server and no third-party account. Everything is written as JSON on your own disk.
