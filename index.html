What this is

A name, not a company. Tessarion Labs is what two people call their own work: the software we build for ourselves first, and the standard we hold it to.

There is no office, no payroll and nothing here to sell you. There are two engineers, a shared backlog, and the hours left over after the day job.

The brand, the written specs and the design system exist because we would rather build one product properly than five in a hurry. That is the whole plan.

Two people and the hours left over is the entire capacity. One product moves at a time and the other waits. That is the trade, and it is why Taimu and Mascada are both still in development.

What we ship

  • Taimu

    Time tracking for independent contractors.

    A macOS menu-bar app. Start the day, pause, resume, end the day — from the menu bar. A monthly calendar shows each day's intervals, a weekly balance per row, and a monthly total against your goal.

    No server and no third-party account. Everything is written as JSON on your own disk.

    Personal finance.
